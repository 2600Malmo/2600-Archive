[@Sergei](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sergei-zaiats/) presented **The Ghost in the Patch**, a talk about using AI-assisted fuzzing to find vulnerabilities left behind by incomplete security patches. He shared his approach to building fuzzing harnesses, investigating known CVEs, and reporting findings responsibly.
